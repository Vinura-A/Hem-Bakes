Our Story, Carefully Folded.

Hem Bakes began as a quiet obsession in a sun-drenched Canberra kitchen. Driven by a desire to bring the uncompromising quality of Parisian patisseries to our community, we spent years perfecting the art of lamination and long-fermentation.

For us, baking is an act of quiet rebellion against the ordinary. We believe that true luxury cannot be rushed. Our doughs rest longer, our ingredients are sourced with meticulous care, and every pastry is finished entirely by hand.

Craftsmanship is our primary ingredient.
